1. Indices and derivative convention
As in Jiang, $\mu,\nu,\lambda,\sigma$ label AOs, $\tilde\nu$ a PAO
identified by its parent AO $\nu$, $i,j,k,l$ active occupied LMOs, and $a,b$
virtual orbitals. We reserve $i_{\rm c}$ for a frozen occupied orbital.
A bar denotes a coefficient of the stationary correlation Lagrangian
differential. For real matrices we use
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pairing}
 \ip{X}{Y}=\sum_{pq}X_{pq}Y_{pq},\qquad
 \sym X=(X+X\T)/2,\qquad d\mathcal L=\sum_X\ip{\bar X}{dX}.
\end{equation}
In particular, off-diagonal elements of a symmetric input occur twice in
this pairing. Let $L_{\mu i}$ be active localized coefficients, $C^{\rm c}_{\mu i_{\rm c}}$
the reference frozen coefficients, and $O=[L\;C^{\rm c}]$. $S,F$ denote
the AO overlap and RHF Fock matrices.
2. Unnormalized projected AO
For a spatial RHF occupied projector each occupied column occurs once.
Define $P=OO\T$. In the AO coefficient representation the raw PAO and its
variation are
\begin{align}
 B_{\mu\tilde\nu}
 &=\delta_{\mu\nu}-\sum_{p\in\mathrm{occ}}\sum_\lambda
 O_{\mu p}O_{\lambda p}S_{\lambda\nu},\label{eq:raw}\\
 dB&=-(dO\,O\T+O\,dO\T)S-P\,dS.\label{eq:db}
\end{align}
For $O\T S O=I$, $O\T S B=0$. The overlap in the rightmost position
matters because $B$ is generally nonsymmetric. If $b$ is one raw column
and $x=b/n$, $n^2=b\T S b>0$, a normalized-column source $g$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:normal}
 \bar b=g/n-Sb(g\T b)/n^3,\qquad
 \bar S_{\rm norm}=-bb\T(g\T b)/(2n^3).
\end{equation}
Apply the actual forward zero-column convention before this rule.
When normalization is differentiated using the diagonal of $B\T S B$,
its source enters the metric block below and must be counted once.
3. Inputs from the pair and triplet contractions
Let $U,W,V,H,D,R,Z$ denote the accumulated sources for, respectively,
$L\T F L$, $L\T F B$, $B\T F B$, $B\T S B$, $B$, $S$, and $SL$.
The last two include the local occupied density-fitting refits.
These definitions specify their normalization by the following scalar
differential, with the sources held fixed:
\begin{align}
 d\mathcal L_{\rm loc}
 &=\ip U{d(L\T F L)}+\ip W{d(L\T F B)}
   +\ip V{d(B\T F B)}+\ip H{d(B\T S B)}\notag\\
 &\quad+\ip D{dB}+\ip R{dS}+\ip Z{d(SL)}.\label{eq:sources}
\end{align}
Thus the total source on the raw PAO coefficients at fixed $L,F,S$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:g}
 G=D+FLW+FB(V+V\T)+SB(H+H\T).
\end{equation}
4. Occupied, frozen, Fock and metric derivatives
Substitution of Eq.~\eqref{eq:db} in Eq.~\eqref{eq:sources} gives
\begin{align}
 \bar L&=-GSL-SG\T L+FL(U+U\T)+FBW\T+SZ,\label{eq:l}\\
 \bar C^{\rm c}&=-GSC^{\rm c}-SG\T C^{\rm c},\label{eq:c}\\
 \bar F&=\sym(LUL\T+LWB\T+BVB\T),\label{eq:f}\\
 \bar S&=\sym(BHB\T+R+ZL\T-PG).\label{eq:s}
\end{align}
For example the individual frozen-column equation reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coreindex}
 \bar C^{\rm c}_{\mu i_{\rm c}}
 =-\sum_{\nu\lambda}
 \left(G_{\mu\tilde\nu}S_{\nu\lambda}
 +S_{\mu\nu}G_{\lambda\tilde\nu}\right)
 C^{\rm c}_{\lambda i_{\rm c}}.
\end{equation}
With no frozen orbitals this is an AO-by-zero block. For an infinitesimal
rotation $dC^{\rm c}_{\mu i_{\rm c}}=C_{\mu a}\,d\kappa_{a i_{\rm c}}$
at fixed active LMOs and AO inputs, the source is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:kappa}
 \frac{\partial\mathcal L_{\rm loc}}{\partial\kappa_{a i_{\rm c}}}
 =\sum_\mu C_{\mu a}\bar C^{\rm c}_{\mu i_{\rm c}}.
\end{equation}
This orbital source is an input to the constrained RHF response problem.
A rotation within the frozen block, $dC^{\rm c}=C^{\rm c}K$ with $K=-K\T$,
leaves its projector invariant by Eq.~\eqref{eq:db}.
5. Implementation and verification
\texttt{occupied\_projection.cc} applies Eqs.~\eqref{eq:l}--\eqref{eq:s}
to a supplied column panel. For example $BX=X-O(O\T SX)$ and
$B\T X=X-SO(O\T X)$. The sources $U,W,V,H,D,R,Z$ correspond in order
to \texttt{ccsd\_foo\_response\_}, the raw \texttt{fov}, \texttt{fvv},
\texttt{overlap}, \texttt{coefficients}, \texttt{df\_refit\_overlap\_bar\_},
and \texttt{df\_sclmo\_bar\_}. The debug probe perturbs one independent
input at fixed geometry and reevaluates all seven products of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:sources}. The separate canonical test rotates the frozen
orbital toward a virtual direction and evaluates the stationary DF-CCSD(T)
correlation Lagrangian. The final nuclear derivative additionally requires
the localization and RHF constraints and the remaining explicit integral
derivatives.

6. Backfill: stationary local correlation intermediates
In the equations below $R_m$ denotes the implemented residual in its own
retained domain, $t_m$ its independent amplitudes, and subscripts on
functions denote partial derivatives. Use the stored ordered doubles
pairing $\tfrac12\sum_{ij}\langle\lambda^{ij},R^{ij}\rangle$ when
converting between independent coordinates and stored multipliers.
For ordinary iterative (T), first eliminate triples, then singles/doubles:
\begin{align}
 R_{3,t_3}\T z_3&=-E_{,t_3},\\
 R_{12,t_{12}}\T z_{12}&=-E_{,t_{12}}-R_{3,t_{12}}\T z_3,\\
 d\mathcal L|_{t}&=dE|_t+\ip{z_3}{dR_3|_t}+\ip{z_{12}}{dR_{12}|_t}.
\end{align}
For a retained projection edge $Y=S_{ij,kl}T^{kl}S_{ij,kl}\T$,
write $S_e=S_{ij,kl}$. Its incoming source $D$ contributes
\begin{align}
 \bar T^{kl}&\mathrel{+}=S_e\T D S_e,&
 \bar S_e&\mathrel{+}=D S_e T^{kl\mathsf T}+D\T S_e T^{kl}.
\end{align}
Transpose the increment on edges that read the transposed amplitude.
For $S_e=X_{ij}\T S_{\rm PAO}X_{kl}$ the three increments are
$S_{\rm PAO}X_{kl}\bar S_e\T$, $X_{ij}\bar S_e X_{kl}\T$ and
$S_{\rm PAO}\T X_{ij}\bar S_e$ respectively.
For a local DF solve $Mb=b_0$, the reverse solve gives
\begin{equation}
 y=M^{-\mathsf T}\bar b,\qquad \bar b_0\mathrel{+}=y,\qquad
 \bar M\mathrel{+}=-\sym(y b\T).
\end{equation}
Apply the same rule to an occupied refit with its actual local overlap metric.
For a fixed-rank spectral projector in an orthonormal parent frame,
$D u_p=n_p u_p$, the mixed retained--discarded variation is
\begin{equation}
 dP=\sum_{p\in r,q\notin r}
 (u_q u_p\T+u_p u_q\T)
 \frac{u_q\T(dD)u_p}{n_p-n_q}.
\end{equation}
This assumes a separated boundary and applies independently at each
selection stage. For any full-minus-retained correction, both energy
derivatives carry their respective forward signs. Accumulate the resulting
sources and projector sources on the first-space LMP2 amplitudes $t^{(2)}$.
With $A=R_{2,t^{(2)}}^{\rm LMP2}$ and their combined source $s$,
\begin{equation}
 A\T z=-s,\qquad d\mathcal L_{\rm LMP2}|_t
 =dE_{\rm explicit}+\ip z{dR^{\rm LMP2}_2|_t}.
\end{equation}

7. Localization and constrained RHF response
Use $L\mapsto L\exp K$, $K_{ji}=k_{ij}=-K_{ij}$ for $i<j$.
For each localized operator $A=L\T A^{\rm AO}L$ put
$w_i=p A_{ii}^{p-1}$, with $p=2$ or $4$ as in the forward objective.
The stationarity residual and correlation source are
\begin{equation}
 g_{ij}=2\sum_A(w_i-w_j)A_{ij},\qquad
 b_{ij}=(L\T\bar L)_{ji}-(L\T\bar L)_{ij}.
\end{equation}
For one operator let $D=AK-KA$, $v_i=p(p-1)A_{ii}^{p-2}D_{ii}$,
$G_{ij}=(w_i-w_j)A_{ij}$. Its exponential-coordinate Hessian action is
\begin{equation}
 (Hk)_{ij}=2(v_i-v_j)A_{ij}+2(w_i-w_j)D_{ij}+(KG-GK)_{ij}.
\end{equation}
Sum over operators and solve $Hz=-b$ at a stationary localization.
For each $i<j$, add $z_{ij}(w_i-w_j)$ to both off-diagonal elements of
$\bar A$. Add $2z_{ij}A_{ij}w'_i$ to diagonal $i$ and subtract
$2z_{ij}A_{ij}w'_j$ from diagonal $j$, where $w'_i=p(p-1)A_{ii}^{p-2}$.
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \bar L\mathrel{+}=2\sum_A A^{\rm AO}L\bar A,\qquad
 \bar A^{\rm AO}=L\bar A L\T .
\end{equation}
A dependent AO operator (in particular an IAO population operator) requires
its own further pullback. In Psi4's canonical occupied-by-virtual CPHF
coordinates define the constraint Jacobian as the implemented
\texttt{cphf\_Hx} action $H_{\rm RHF}$. For a source $b^{\rm RHF}$
in these same coordinates the adjoint satisfies
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm RHF}z^{\rm RHF}=-b^{\rm RHF},\qquad
 r=H_{\rm RHF}z^{\rm RHF}+b^{\rm RHF}.
\end{equation}
All occupied rows participate, including frozen occupied rows.
8. Molecular canonical RHF source
Let $C=[C_{\rm c},C_{\rm o}]$ contain the canonical frozen and active
occupied orbitals, $V$ all virtual orbitals, and $U=C_{\rm o}\T SL$.
Return the localization-relaxed source to canonical active columns as
$B_{\rm o}=\bar L U\T$ and write $B_{\rm c}=\bar C^{\rm c}$.
For a core--active rotation $dC_{\rm c}=C_{\rm o}k$,
$dC_{\rm o}=-C_{\rm c}k\T$, maintain the canonical off-block constraint:
\begin{align}
 k_{ic}&=-\frac{(C_{\rm o}\T dF C_{\rm c})_{ic}}{\epsilon_i-\epsilon_c},&
 M_{ic}&=\frac{(C_{\rm o}\T B_{\rm c}-B_{\rm o}\T C_{\rm c})_{ic}}
 {\epsilon_i-\epsilon_c},\\
 F_*&=\bar F-\sym(C_{\rm o} M C_{\rm c}\T).
\end{align}
At fixed geometry $dC=-Vx\T$ in the Psi4 CPHF convention. Let
$B=[B_{\rm c},B_{\rm o}]+B_{\rm IAO}$ with the last source present for IBO.
For the spatial density $D=CC\T$ the Fock derivative is
$dF=2J[dD]-K[dD]$. Self-adjointness of the Coulomb and exchange maps gives
\begin{equation}
 b^{\rm RHF}=-B\T V-2C\T(2J[F_*]-K[F_*])V .
\end{equation}
When no core is frozen, the mixed block is empty.
8.1. Occupied dependence of IAO operators
With primary--minimal overlaps $S_{12}$ and minimal metric $S_{22}$,
define the following full-rank intermediates:
\begin{align}
 T&=S^{-1}S_{12}S_{22}^{-1}S_{12}\T,& X&=TC,&
 H&=(X\T S X)^{-1},& D_t&=XHX\T,\\
 P&=S^{-1}+2DSD_t-D-D_t,& Y&=PS_{12},&
 N&=(Y\T S Y)^{-1/2},& I&=YN.
\end{align}
For atom $A$ let $I_A$ be its selected IAO columns; the forward operator
is $S I_A I_A\T S$. The localization operator multiplier $\bar A$ gives
$\bar I_A=2SL\bar A L\T S I_A$. For $Y\T S Y=W\operatorname{diag}(r_i^2)W\T$,
the reverse equations at fixed AO metrics are
\begin{align}
 E_{ij}&=-1/[r_i r_j(r_i+r_j)],\\
 Q&=W\big[E\circ(W\T\sym(Y\T\bar I)W)\big]W\T,\\
 \bar P&=(\bar I N+2SYQ)S_{12}\T,\\
 \bar D&=2\bar P D_t S-\bar P,&
 \bar D_t&=2SD\bar P-\bar P,\\
 B_{\rm IAO}&=(\bar D+\bar D\T)C+
 T\T(1-SD_t)(\bar D_t+\bar D_t\T)XH .
\end{align}
This occupied derivative applies at fixed primary and minimal overlaps;
their explicit nuclear derivatives enter a later Cartesian contraction.
9. Cartesian contraction for Boys and Mulliken PM
Let $s=S^{A\alpha}$, $f=F^{A\alpha}|_{D}$ and $D=CC\T$. Use occupied
transport $dC=-\tfrac12DsC$ and the corresponding virtual transport with
occupied component $-DsV$. The reference density transport is $dD=-DsD$.
Let $m=F_*-\bar F$ be the frozen--active multiplier and put
\begin{align}
 Z&=C z^{\rm RHF}V\T,& B_F&=\bar F+m+\sym Z,&
 P_F&=2J[B_F]-K[B_F].
\end{align}
The metric coefficient before the explicit localization operators is
\begin{align}
 B_S=\bar S-\sym\big[
 \tfrac12 C B\T CC\T + C\epsilon_{\rm occ}z^{\rm RHF}V\T
 +\tfrac12(DFm+mFD)+DP_F D\big].
\end{align}
Here $B$ contains the independent occupied coefficient sources after
localization. The Cartesian contribution of orbital, Fock and overlap terms is
\begin{equation}
 g_{A\alpha}^{\rm orb}=\ip{B_F}{f}+\ip{B_S}{s}
 +\sum_K\ip{L\bar A_K L\T}{(A_K^{\rm AO})^{A\alpha}}.
\end{equation}
Boys uses the three AO dipole derivatives. For Mulliken PM with atom selector
$E_K$, the last operator derivative is $(E_Ks+sE_K)/2$.
For the water verification calculation define $Q$ as the reference JKFIT
three-center integrals with flattened AO pair columns, $J_0$ its Coulomb
metric and $b=J_0^{-1}Q$. The fitted ERI derivative at fixed coefficients is
\begin{align}
 I'&={Q'}\T b+b\T Q'-b\T J_0' b,\\
 f_{\mu\nu}&=h'_{\mu\nu}+\sum_{\lambda\sigma}D_{\lambda\sigma}
 [2 I'_{\mu\nu,\lambda\sigma}-I'_{\mu\lambda,\nu\sigma}].
\end{align}
Add $g^{\rm orb}$ to the RHF nuclear gradient and the previously derived
RIFIT metric and three-center contractions for CCSD, ordinary (T), the PNO
projector, truncation correction and LMP2 response. The dense ERI expression
above is restricted to the small-system verification code.

9.1. Screened SCF Fock derivative using the RHF gradient kernel
Let $\mathcal E'(X)=\langle X,2J'[X]-K'[X]\rangle$ denote one Cartesian
derivative of the RHF two-electron energy at fixed spatial density $X$.
For a column panel $E$ of the AO identity and $R=B_F E$, put
$L_\pm=(E\pm R)/2$, $P_\pm=L_\pm L_\pm\T$. Then
\begin{equation}
 P_+-P_-=\sym(RE\T),\qquad \sum_{\rm panels}(P_+-P_-)=B_F.
\end{equation}
Bilinearity gives the exchange and Coulomb contraction for one panel:
\begin{align}
 \langle P_+-P_-,2J'[D]-K'[D]\rangle
 =\tfrac12\big(&\mathcal E'(D+P_+)-\mathcal E'(P_+)\notag\\
 &-\mathcal E'(D+P_-)+\mathcal E'(P_-)\big).
\end{align}
The four arguments are represented by $[C,L_+]$, $L_+$, $[C,L_-]$ and
$L_-$, respectively, permitting direct use of the existing restricted
DFJKGrad kernel. The one-electron term is
$\langle P_+-P_-,h'\rangle$. Each kernel call computes all Cartesian
components together with the shell-pair screening and metric conditioning
of the DF-SCF gradient.
10. Screened dipole and crude semicanonical-MP2 corrections
\label{sec:screened-gradient}
This section records the implementation in commit \texttt{6a517440}.
The closed-shell RHF gradient-model energy now includes both estimates:
\begin{align}
 E_{\rm grad}&=E_{\rm RHF}+E_{\rm retained\ CCSD}+E_{\rm iterative\ (T)}
 +\Delta E_{\rm PNO}^{\rm first}+\Delta E_{\rm PNO}^{\rm final}\notag\\
 &\quad+\sum_{\{ij\}\in\mathcal C}E_{\{ij\}}^{\rm SC}
       +\sum_{\{ij\}\in\mathcal D}E_{\{ij\}}^{\rm dip}.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-functional}
\end{align}
The disjoint sets $\mathcal D$ and $\mathcal C$ contain, respectively,
pairs excluded at dipole/DOI prescreening and pairs subsequently excluded
by crude semicanonical MP2. Each contains unique off-diagonal pairs $i<j$.
Their membership, AO/PAO/auxiliary lists and parent pivot spans are fixed
while the electronic orbitals and integrals respond. No derivative of a
threshold decision is an energy term on this smooth branch. Every surviving
pair remains strong, and all allowed triplets use the common TNO cutoff;
ordinary/model energy agreement also requires matching those choices.

10.1. Pair counting and the corrected dipole energy
For one pair put $o=f_{ii}+f_{jj}$, with $f_{ii}=L_i\T F L_i$.
In orthonormal local virtual parents define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal A_{ij}(t)=o t-F_i t-t F_j=K,\qquad
 t_{ab}=\frac{K_{ab}}{o-\epsilon_a-\epsilon_b}
 \quad\hbox{at the semicanonical reference}.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-amplitudes}
\end{equation}
For crude SC-MP2 both virtual slots use the same pair parent,
$F_i=F_j=F_p$, and $\mathcal S(t)=2t-t\T$. Native storage contains
\begin{equation}
 e_{ij}^{\rm SC}=\ip K{\mathcal S(t)},\qquad
 e_{ji}^{\rm SC}=e_{ij}^{\rm SC},\qquad
 E_{\{ij\}}^{\rm SC}=2\ip K{\mathcal S(t)}.
 \label{eq:gradient-sc-counting}
\end{equation}
Its existing sum over ordered pairs was correct.

The distant dipole estimate uses different parents $Y_i,Y_j$, the three
electronic AO dipole matrices $\mu_k$, and the forward sign convention:
\begin{align}
 r_{i,k}&=L_i\T\mu_k L_i,& d_{i,ka}&=L_i\T\mu_k Y_{i,a},\\
 \mathbf R&=\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j,& \rho&=\mathbf R\T\mathbf R,\\
 A(\mathbf R)&=\rho^{-3/2}I-3\rho^{-5/2}\mathbf R\mathbf R\T,&
 K&=d_i\T A(\mathbf R)d_j,\\
 E_{\{ij\}}^{\rm dip}&=4\ip Kt
 =-4\sum_{ab}\frac{K_{ab}^2}{\epsilon_a+\epsilon_b-o}.&&
 \label{eq:gradient-dipole-counting}
\end{align}
This is already the complete unique-pair estimate, consistent with Werner,
J. Chem. Phys. 145, 201101 (2016), Eqs. 1, 3 and 4,
doi:10.1063/1.4968595. The old code mirrored this value into $ij$ and $ji$
and added both. The corrected accumulator adds only $i<j$. The stored
estimate, its screening bound and the screening test are unchanged.
The bound selects the branch; it is not substituted for this energy.
The shared counting fix also affects the corresponding DOCC--DOCC energy
path in open-shell calculations; the response derived here is RHF only.

10.2. Amplitude adjoints and Fock sources
Use the local scalar Lagrangian
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_p=E_p+\ip z{K-\mathcal A_{ij}(t)}.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-lagrangian}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal L_p$ is only a notation for this scalar; its residual sign
is $K-\mathcal A_{ij}(t)$. With symmetric real virtual Fock matrices the
operator is self-adjoint. In the SC-MP2 case it commutes with transpose.
Eliminating the amplitude derivative gives
\begin{align}
 z_{\rm SC}&=2\mathcal S(t),& \bar K_{\rm SC}&=4\mathcal S(t),\\
 \bar F_p&=\sym(zt\T+t\T z),& \bar o_{\rm SC}&=-\ip zt,\\
 z_{\rm dip}&=4t,& \bar K_{\rm dip}&=8t,\\
 \bar F_i&=\sym(zt\T),& \bar F_j&=\sym(t\T z),\qquad
 \bar o_{\rm dip}=-\ip zt.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-bars}
\end{align}
Both occupied diagonals receive $\bar f_{ii}\mathrel{+}=\bar o$ and
$\bar f_{jj}\mathrel{+}=\bar o$. For example the dipole differential is
\begin{equation}
 dE_{\{ij\}}^{\rm dip}=\ip{\bar K}{dK}+\ip{\bar F_i}{dF_i}
                  +\ip{\bar F_j}{dF_j}+\bar o\,do.
\end{equation}
Keep the full virtual Fock covectors, including their off-diagonal entries.
They include the effects otherwise attributed to semicanonical frame
rotation, without dividing by within-space virtual energy differences.
The code rejects amplitude denominators with magnitude at most $10^{-12}$.

10.3. Crude local density fitting and occupied refits
For an excluded SC-MP2 pair let $Q_i,Q_j$ be its original crude panels,
$J$ its local Coulomb metric and $X$ its PAO-to-orthonormal-parent matrix.
Then
\begin{align}
 b_i&=J^{-1}Q_i,& b_j&=J^{-1}Q_j,& K_0&=Q_i\T b_j,\\
 K&=X\T K_0 X,& G&=X\bar K X\T.&&
\end{align}
The reverse local fitting equations are
\begin{equation}
 \bar Q_i=b_jG\T,\qquad \bar Q_j=b_iG,\qquad
 \bar J=-\sym(b_iG b_j\T).
 \label{eq:gradient-crude-fit-bars}
\end{equation}
Each auxiliary function $q$ uses the actual occupied AO set $A_q$ and
virtual AO set $B_q$ of its center. Writing $N$ for normalized PAOs and
$v$ for a PAO label in the pair domain,
\begin{align}
 S_{A_qA_q}\widetilde L_i^{,q}&=(SL_i)_{A_q},\\
 (Q_i)_{qv}&=\sum_{\mu\in A_q,\nu\in B_q}
       \widetilde L_{\mu i}^{,q}(q|\mu\nu)N_{\nu v}.
 \label{eq:gradient-crude-panel}
\end{align}
The integral sum respects the native AO screening mask. Reverse this
product into AO three-center, normalized-PAO and fitted-LMO covectors.
For an incoming fitted-LMO source $\overline{\widetilde L}^{,q}$,
\begin{equation}
 Z_q=S_{A_qA_q}^{-\mathsf T}\overline{\widetilde L}^{,q},\qquad
 \overline{SL}_{A_q}\mathrel{+}=Z_q,\qquad
 \bar S_{A_qA_q}\mathrel{+}=-\sym(Z_q\widetilde L^{q\mathsf T}).
 \label{eq:gradient-crude-refit-bars}
\end{equation}
The two overlap contributions are distinct: one belongs to the local
inverse and one to its right-hand side $SL$.

The native code performs these contractions before the crude domains are
replaced by refined ones. Explicit metric and three-center nuclear terms
are contracted at that stage; the remaining sparse orbital/Fock/metric
sources are saved for the common reverse pass. Using later refined lists
would differentiate a different energy.

10.4. Local virtual parent and normalized PAO response
Let $N$ denote the relevant normalized PAO columns and $M=N\T S N$.
On the fixed, full-rank Cholesky pivot span, $X\T M X=I$. For any
projected operator $A_X=X\T A_0X$, its sources are
\begin{equation}
 \bar A_0=X\bar A_X X\T,\qquad
 \bar X\mathrel{+}=A_0X\bar A_X\T+A_0\T X\bar A_X.
\end{equation}
Combine all sources on $X$ before applying the parent-metric rule
\begin{equation}
 dX=-\tfrac12 X(X\T dM X),\qquad
 \bar M=-\tfrac12 X\sym(X\T\bar X)X\T.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-parent}
\end{equation}
This choice fixes a transport gauge; the scalar energy is invariant under
internal rotations of its complete selected parent. A rank reduction
inside the selected pivot block requires additional response and is
rejected by the current parent routine.

Apply the same rules to $F_p=X\T N\T F N X$ and to the separate dipole
parents. The induced $N\T F N$ and $N\T S N$ sources join the common PAO
Fock and overlap blocks. Direct moment/DF coefficient sources join the
normalized-PAO coefficient block. Reverse PAO normalization with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:normal} before using the raw-PAO formulas
\eqref{eq:g}--\eqref{eq:s}. The occupied projector includes frozen core.
Thus frozen orbitals contribute through PAO motion even though the
screened occupied pair indices themselves are active LMOs.

10.5. Transition moments, centroids and explicit dipole integrals
The dipole coupling gives
\begin{equation}
 \bar d_i=A d_j\bar K\T,\qquad
 \bar d_j=A\T d_i\bar K,\qquad
 \bar A=d_i\bar K d_j\T.
\end{equation}
Differentiating $A(\mathbf R)$ yields
\begin{align}
 \overline{\mathbf R}
 &=-3\rho^{-5/2}\operatorname{tr}(\bar A)\mathbf R
   -3\rho^{-5/2}(\bar A+\bar A\T)\mathbf R\notag\\
 &\quad+15\rho^{-7/2}(\mathbf R\T\bar A\mathbf R)\mathbf R,\\
 \overline{\mathbf r}_i&=\overline{\mathbf R},\qquad
 \overline{\mathbf r}_j=-\overline{\mathbf R}.
 \label{eq:gradient-centroid-bar}
\end{align}
For either endpoint write $b_k=(\bar d_{k:})\T$, with $Y=NX$ for its
own local parent. The moment/centroid contributions are
\begin{align}
 \bar L^{\rm direct}&\mathrel{+}=\sum_k
       \{\mu_kYb_k+2\bar r_k\mu_kL\},\\
 \bar Y^{\rm moment}&=\sum_k\mu_kL b_k\T,\\
 \bar\mu_k&\mathrel{+}=L(Yb_k)\T+\bar r_kLL\T,\\
 \bar N&\mathrel{+}=\bar Y^{\rm moment}X\T,\qquad
 \bar X\mathrel{+}=N\T\bar Y^{\rm moment}.
 \label{eq:gradient-dipole-moment-bars}
\end{align}
The virtual Fock contributions are handled by the preceding parent rules.
Symmetrizing $\bar\mu_k$ is optional because the AO dipole derivative is
symmetric. Sum these increments over each unique excluded pair once.
The electronic dipole operators are used for both Boys and PM references;
the localization objective does not change the dipole energy formula.

10.6. Combination with the total Cartesian gradient
Let $B_{\rm dip}$ collect the direct occupied covectors and $D_k$ the AO
dipole covectors. Extend Eq.~\eqref{eq:sources} by
\begin{equation}
 d\mathcal L_{\rm loc}\mathrel{+}=\ip{B_{\rm dip}}{dL}
                    +\sum_k\ip{D_k}{d\mu_k},\qquad
 \bar L\mathrel{+}=B_{\rm dip}.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-assembly-source}
\end{equation}
All screened-pair sources participate in the existing localization,
frozen--active and RHF adjoints. Therefore $g^{\rm orb}$ above must be
formed from the combined sources. With $\mathcal F$ containing CCSD,
ordinary (T), PNO projector, PNO truncation, LMP2 response and excluded
SC-MP2 fitting contributions, the complete gradient is
\begin{equation}
 g_{A\alpha}=g_{A\alpha}^{\rm RHF}+g_{A\alpha}^{\rm orb}
 +\sum_{s\in\mathcal F}\left(g_{A\alpha,s}^{\rm metric}
                         +g_{A\alpha,s}^{\rm three\ center}\right)
 +\sum_k\ip{D_k}{\mu_k^{A\alpha}}.
 \label{eq:gradient-screened-total}
\end{equation}
The last term is the explicit dipole-energy derivative. For Boys, the
localization-constraint dipole derivative already contained in
$g^{\rm orb}$ has a different multiplier and is also required. Neither
term replaces the other. The screened orbital response is already present
in the combined adjoints and must not be added a second time.

In code the extra arrays, each in hartree/bohr, are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l}
\texttt{DLPNO SC-LMP2 PAIRS AUXILIARY METRIC GRADIENT}\\
\texttt{DLPNO SC-LMP2 PAIRS THREE-CENTER GRADIENT}\\
\texttt{DLPNO DIPOLE PAIRS EXPLICIT GRADIENT}
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The first two contain only explicit fitting derivatives at fixed electronic
data; the third contains only explicit AO dipole derivatives. None is a
standalone fully relaxed correction gradient. Their orbital response is
carried by the common adjoints described above.

10.7. Validation and derivative scope
Nonzero screened corrections were tested on four H$_2$/cc-pVDZ molecules
with Boys and Mulliken PM, and three LiH/def2-SVP molecules with Boys and
three frozen Li $1s$ orbitals. Each fixture has one excluded dipole pair
and two excluded SC-MP2 pairs. Two selected total-gradient coordinates per
fixture agree with independently reconverged energy differences at
$\pm0.004/\pm0.002$ bohr, Richardson extrapolated, with maximum errors
$4.09\times10^{-11}$, $5.91\times10^{-11}$ and $3.21\times10^{-10}$
hartree/bohr, respectively. The acceptance threshold is $2\times10^{-8}$.
Native domains, pair labels, retained ranks and physical parent spans are
checked on the displaced branch; net force and torque checks also pass.
The CTest regression reconstructs the unique dipole energy to catch a
return of the counting error and verifies SC-MP2 ordered multiplicity.

The native analytic gradient uses the existing AO-screened integral
contractions. An optional snapshot retained with
\texttt{DLPNO\_KEEP\_LMP2\_RESPONSE} supplies original crude domains,
parents and panels for independent mixed-response checks; the gradient
itself does not require this option. The separate analytic correction
curvature kernels currently require the AO Schwarz screen off and do not
complete the screened PNO/CCSD molecular Hessian providers. Public analytic
Hessian routing remains disabled. These are fixed-branch derivatives;
threshold crossings, open-shell gradients, complete-gradient linear
scaling and a general screened Hessian are not established by these tests.
